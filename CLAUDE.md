# Pre-Call Sales Intelligence Routine — Standing Instructions

## Slack Delivery Rules

- **Only post to `#lead-briefs`.** Never fall back to `#general`, `#sales`, or any other channel.
- If `#lead-briefs` does not exist, **do not post anywhere**. Instead:
  - Send a push notification stating: "⚠️ #lead-briefs channel not found. Briefs were not posted. Please create the channel and re-run."
  - Do not silently fall back to another channel.
